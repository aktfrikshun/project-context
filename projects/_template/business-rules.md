# Business rules

Use stable IDs such as `BR-001`. A rule states who or what it applies to, its condition, required outcome, exceptions, source, and examples. Unknown rules remain open questions; they are never filled by assumption.

## BR-001 — Rule name

- **Status:** proposed | active | deprecated
- **Statement:** When [condition], [actor/system] must [outcome].
- **Rationale:** Why the domain requires this.
- **Applies to:** Scope and actors.
- **Exceptions:** Explicit exceptions, or `None documented`.
- **Source/owner:** Authoritative policy or accountable person.
- **Effective date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Examples:** Representative valid and invalid cases.
- **Related:** Workflows, glossary terms, tests, or ADRs.

See [the business-rule template](../../templates/business-rules-template.md).
