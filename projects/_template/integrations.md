# Integrations

## INT-001 — Integration name

- **Purpose:** Capability supplied or consumed.
- **Direction:** inbound | outbound | bidirectional
- **Owner:** Internal and external owners.
- **Contract:** API, event, file, or human procedure; link authoritative specification.
- **Data classification:** Categories transferred; never include real secrets or personal data.
- **Authentication/authorization:** Mechanism and trust boundary.
- **Reliability:** Timeouts, retries, idempotency, rate limits, ordering, and recovery.
- **Environments:** Endpoints described symbolically, not secret values.
- **Observability:** Health signals and support route.
- **Lifecycle:** Versioning, compatibility, and deprecation.

See [the integration template](../../templates/integration-template.md).
