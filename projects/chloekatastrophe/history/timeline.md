- **Childhood:** Raised in Omsk by Gregor and Isabella. English is the home language; Russian comes through Gregor, school, friends, family, and daily life. Regular South Jersey visits make America a second home.

- **2026 / age 24:** Current public archive frame; Daughter of Echoes and the reconstruction are active.
