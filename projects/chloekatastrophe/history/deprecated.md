# Deprecated and Superseded Information

## Older visual baseline

The standalone Chloe repo and early marketing material describe Chloe as 5'4" / 163 cm, 118 lb, with emerald-green eyes, flawless porcelain skin without freckles, straight jet-black hair, and fashion-model proportions. An even older 116 lb value also appears in historical material.

**Status:** superseded for new production by Chloe Model v1: approximately 5'7" / 170 cm, gray-green eyes with amber flecks, fair natural skin with freckles, dark chestnut-to-nearly-black wavy hair, and balanced realistic hourglass proportions. Historical assets remain valid archive evidence and may be used only when the older style is explicitly requested.

Sources: `chloe-katastrophe/canon/CANON_FACTS.md`, `chloe-katastrophe/canon/CANON_DECISIONS.md`; `frikshun_marketing/archives/chloe-katastrophe/canon/CANON_DECISIONS.md`; `frikshun_image_studio/studio/reference-packs/chloe_model_v1/MODEL_CARD.md`

## Earlier family wording

Statements calling Isabella simply Italian or implying Chloe learned English after immigration are superseded. Isabella is American-born and Italian by heritage; English was Chloe's primary home language and she grew up bilingual.

Source: marketing archive `canon/CANON_DECISIONS.md` (2026-07-17)

## Earlier Gregor wording

The standalone repo's statement that Gregor “often worked as a consultant to the KGB” conflicts with and is superseded in current marketing canon by a civilian critical-infrastructure scientist/systems-engineer role. No current source supplies an agency name or supports weapon/combat work.

Sources: `chloe-katastrophe/canon/CANON_FACTS.md`; marketing archive `canon/GREGOR_CANON_RECONCILIATION_2026-07-14.md`

## Product scope shifts

The initial first-version plan deferred external API publishing and real AI chat. Creator OS later implemented several publisher integrations and FrikFan implemented provider-backed conversational agents. The original day-one boundary remains planning history, not current implementation status.
