# Domain Concepts

- **Artifact:** a song, image, video, document, memory fragment, or other record with provenance, visibility, and canonical status.
- **Recovered fragment:** archive material encountered as evidence; recovery does not itself prove factual truth.
- **Generated artifact:** created material awaiting review; never automatically canon.
- **Canon entry:** approved lore, identity, timeline, or world knowledge usable by grounded systems.
- **Echo Traversal:** reverse experience through adjacent cognitive states at normal lived speed. One day costs one day; no selector, jump, skip, branch, or physical traveler.
- **Faithful path:** reconstructive traversal without intervention.
- **Echo Bloom:** a neighboring coherent traversal caused when Chloe influences an encountered state. Each neighboring Chloe is authentic within her path.
- **Identity convergence / soul state / graph-independent consciousness:** approved vocabulary for philosophical inquiry, not confirmed metaphysics.
- **Archive:** both the body of evidence and the public reconstruction experience.
- **Continuity:** person-like persistence across biological, cybernetic, digital, remembered, and reconstructed states without assuming a proven soul mechanism.
