# Business and Canon Rules

1. Every meaningful item carries a status: confirmed canon, approved decision/model, draft canon, proposal, generated artifact, recovered fragment, contradiction, unresolved mystery, or deprecated material.
2. Only approved/reference canon is usable for generation by default; unresolved mysteries are excluded.
3. Public visibility and canonical approval are separate decisions.
4. Generated media accidents and fan theories never become canon automatically.
5. Chloe Model v1 governs new visual production unless an older archive style is explicitly requested.
6. Public archive and private creator operations remain separate surfaces.
7. The public archive exposes approved artifacts; private drafts, credentials, and operational data remain private.
8. Rails/public archive owns durable shared artifact/release/search schema; Creator OS treats it as a contract.
9. Ordinary social posts should be native and conversational, not burdened by compulsory promotional footers; dedicated participation messages carry broader invitations.
10. Artistic ambiguity cannot override disclosure, consent, payment, safety, or privacy requirements.
