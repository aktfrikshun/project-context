# Narrative Archives

## Repositories

- `chloe-katastrophe`: original dedicated canon repository; strongest for June 7 quick facts, early visual boards, character sheets, and workflow discipline.
- `frikshun_marketing/archives/chloe-katastrophe`: more current narrative/public archive, accepted decisions through July 2026, music, public site, origin models, and marketing workflows.

The marketing archive contains approved surname continuity, current family/origin refinements, Gregor reconciliation, traversal models, public identity, and the explicit Chloe Model v1 alignment. The dedicated repo preserves older canonical wording and assets and should remain historical evidence rather than being silently treated as current.

Important paths: `canon/`, `characters/`, `stories/`, `brand/`, `music/`, `visuals/`, `workflows/`, and `site/`.

The public marketing archive is the preferred home for fuller artifact records: provenance, reconstruction context, contradictions, extended reflection, related evidence, and unresolved questions. Social channels should normally carry a shorter, emotionally complete signal that can stand alone and, when useful, lead interested readers to the deeper archive record. Do not force every social post to summarize the full archive entry.

Archive cadence should track social cadence. Each day with social publishing should create or update a dated artifact record, field note, daily signal log, or collaboration record. Several lightweight posts may roll up into one daily signal log; meaningful artifacts or developments retain standalone records. The complete post-and-interaction ledger remains private in Creator OS, while the public archive preserves the durable narrative and learning.

Promote accepted cross-project canon here; keep release kits, raw prompts, site code, and individual production artifacts in their source repository.
