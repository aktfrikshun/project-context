## Generation acceptance gate

Reject an output if any of these failures occur:

- A prosthesis appears on Chloe's anatomical right side.
- A frontal or near-frontal image places the prostheses on the viewer's left.
- Any portion of the left leg from hip through toes is depicted as natural, or the prosthesis begins at the upper thigh, knee, or lower leg.
- Additional cybernetic anatomy is invented.
- The artificial left eye is placed on the wrong side or rendered as conspicuous machinery.

For rear or strongly rotated views, validate anatomical laterality rather than viewer-relative position.

## Prohibited current use

- Inventing unseen rear surfaces, hidden mechanisms, surgical interfaces, scars, or engineering explanations.
- Exaggerating the artificial left eye into a conspicuous glowing lens, visible mechanical socket, or facial redesign.
- Adding other cybernetic anatomy without a separately approved reference or decision.
- Treating task wording, chat summaries, historical prompts, or generated images as approved visual evidence.
