**Status:** Accepted model and creative boundary

**Date:** 2026-07-15

## Context

Earlier material left traversal, influence, branching, consciousness, and real-world analogies vulnerable to convenient or overconfident explanation.

Faithful traversal reconstructs; intervention may produce an Echo Bloom neighboring path. Event topology, identity convergence, soul state, and graph-independent consciousness remain models for inquiry, not proven metaphysics. Real medical, psychiatric, religious, and extraordinary experiences must never be claimed as explained by the fiction.
