# ADR-003: Preserve Status and Provenance

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The Chloe universe intentionally mixes public record, recovered memory, fiction, generated media, testimony, and contradiction. Flattening them destroys both reliability and mystery.

## Decision

Every meaningful item must retain a status and source. Confirmed canon, draft canon, generated artifact, recovered fragment, contradiction, unresolved mystery, current implementation, proposal, and deprecated material are not interchangeable. Generated work requires explicit promotion.

## Consequences

Retrieval and generation must filter by status; human review remains necessary; conflicting claims stay visible until evidence or an approved decision resolves them.
