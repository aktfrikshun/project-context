# ADR-005: Version Cybernetic Chloe as an Additive Reference Pack

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-07-27

## Context

Future cybernetic continuity exists in narrative canon. Allen supplied and approved three still images and one motion study on 2026-07-27, providing the evidence needed to ground Cybernetic Chloe's visual design.

## Decision

Maintain Cybernetic Chloe v1 as a versioned, additive visual pack layered on Chloe Model v1. Preserve the base identity while recording every approved cybernetic reference by stable asset ID, checksum, provenance, status, permitted uses, demonstrated features, limitations, and relationship to Chloe Model v1.

## Consequences

- Chloe Model v1 remains required for cybernetic queries and generation.
- Cybernetic details come only from approved pack evidence or explicit accepted decisions.
- Hidden and conflicting image details remain unresolved.
- Generated images and historical prompts cannot promote themselves into the pack.

## Approval record

Allen explicitly identified the supplied FoxyAI media as Cybernetic Chloe canon on 2026-07-27. The reviewed manifest records what each asset demonstrates and what remains unresolved.
