# Roadmap

## Current priority

Find a repeatable path from strangers to returning participants. Publish and measure reusable discovery formats, participate in adjacent communities, collaborate with small trusted creators, and improve software only where observed work justifies it.

## Near-term product work

- Keep one canonical destination clear and useful.
- Maintain a measurable experiment log.
- Complete the public archive/search experience and its durable data contract.
- Improve review-first content operations and fan-comment workflows.

## Deferred or conditional

- Automated publishing to additional platforms.
- Dedicated community features before an active community exists.
- Podcast pilot only after a returning audience exists.
- Audiobook after the public biography stabilizes; Russian audio after native-speaker editorial review.
- Hosted multi-creator Creator OS only after a deliberate product decision.
- Large lore expansions and speculative features that do not improve discovery, measurement, or retention.
