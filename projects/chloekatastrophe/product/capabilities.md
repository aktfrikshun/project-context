# Capabilities

## Current implementations

- Creator OS imports approved archive material, excluding unresolved mysteries from generation by default.
- It uploads/analyzes artifacts; creates and approves platform-native drafts; publishes to supported channels; records attempts; and polls metrics/comments.
- Its daily-fragment path creates shared media plus differentiated captions for Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, and FanVue.
- TikTok reel generation is review-first and does not auto-publish.
- The early public Rails application owns the intended public archive/search surface and shared durable schema.
- FrikFan interactive agents share a Chloe persona layer while retaining role-specific platform expertise.

## Planned or experimental

- Full searchable public artifact and release archive.
- Fan-safe Chloe chat grounded in approved canon.
- Campaign/calendar workflows, richer funnel analytics, and additional publishers.
- Possible multi-creator hosted Creator OS.
- Semantic search with pgvector.

Implementation status is project-local and can change independently of canon.
