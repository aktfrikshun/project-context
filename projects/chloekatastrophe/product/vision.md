# Product Vision

The Chloe ecosystem has two connected surfaces:

1. A public ChloKat archive: searchable artifacts, current news, releases, lore, approved fragments, and canon-safe exploration for fans.
2. A private FrikShun Creator OS: artifact intake, canon-aware transformations, platform drafts, campaigns, publishing assistance, and analytics.

Chloe is the first resident of a system that may later support other fictional or virtual artists. The immediate product promise is to reduce creative friction while preserving continuity: create once, adapt natively, review, publish, and lead interested people deeper into the archive.

Music distribution remains with DistroKid and streaming platforms; the ecosystem stores metadata and links rather than replacing distribution.
