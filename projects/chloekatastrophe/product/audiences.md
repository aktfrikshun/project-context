# Audiences

- **Fans and curious listeners:** discover Chloe through self-contained human tensions, music, visual identity, and mystery.
- **Returning participants and superfans:** search the archive, connect fragments, discuss theories, follow releases, and join recurring story arcs.
- **Allen / FrikShun:** approve canon and assets, operate publishing workflows, supply the human archivist perspective, and evaluate results.
- **Approved collaborators:** contribute production, cultural review, evidence, and creative work without silently changing canon.
- **Developers and AI agents:** build and operate canon-aware systems from documented boundaries.
- **Potential future creators:** a proposed audience if Creator OS becomes a hosted multi-creator product.

Cold audiences should not be asked to understand the whole mythology before receiving a reason to care. FanVue is a depth-and-funding destination for already interested people, not the primary cold-audience entry point.
