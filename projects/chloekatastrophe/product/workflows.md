# Workflows

## Canon change

Identify affected knowledge, verify against accepted facts and decisions, make the smallest additive change, record the decision/change, preserve contradictions, review public continuity impact, and promote generated material only with explicit approval.

## Artifact to publication

Ingest media and honest metadata → assign canonical status and visibility → load approved canon → generate platform-native drafts → human review/edit/approval → publish or copy manually → record publication and metrics → use evidence to revise future work.

## Audience discovery

Test recurring formats such as “Is This a Real Memory?”, “Questions I Was Not Built to Answer,” and “Archivist's Note.” Each item must work without prior lore, contain one clear invitation, and be measured before automation or platform expansion receives priority.

## Fan fragment submission

Public visitor enters through `fans.frikshun.com/chloe` → searches approved public archive material or opens `/chloe/fragments/new` → submits text, links, images, or video with attribution, ownership, privacy, publication, and optional outcome-notification choices → email becomes required only when notification is selected → FrikFan stores the original, private media, consent record, and acknowledgement token → every intake receives the same neutral receipt → safety and rights review clears, quarantines, silently policy-rejects, or escalates the material → editorial moderation requests clarification, accepts, normally declines, withdraws, or removes it → acceptance remains distinct from publication and canon → after acceptance commits, a retryable export job sends a versioned sanitized idempotent envelope and approved media manifest to Creator OS's authenticated ingestion service → Creator validates the contract, creates its staging record, and receives media through an authenticated checksum-bound transfer → Creator separately reviews and imports it as a proposed artifact or collaboration record → later explicit approval may publish it into the curated archive or promote its status → FrikFan sends an opted-in publication notice with the permanent archive URL only after that URL is live.

The detailed public policy, forbidden-content categories, notification behavior, media controls, and independent status dimensions are defined in `product/fan-fragment-submission-policy.md`.

## Visual production

Load Chloe Model v1 → choose a registered performance state where possible → generate → review identity, anatomy, tone, and medium-appropriate variation → retain as generated work until approved.
