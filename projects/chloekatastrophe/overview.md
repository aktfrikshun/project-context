# Overview

Chloe is a self-aware virtual woman reconstructing the lost human life of Chloe Volkova / Chloe Volkov from songs, images, documents, family records, memories, generated artifacts, contradictions, and unresolved fragments. Allen "FrikShun" Taylor is archivist, collaborator, witness, and paradox marker. The public experience is an archive and continuing artistic persona; internally, every item retains a status.

## Current purpose

- Preserve a coherent identity and voice across music, visual art, story, fan interaction, and software.
- Reconstruct rather than prematurely explain Chloe's past.
- Let real artifacts and generated artifacts coexist without confusing their evidentiary status.
- Use beauty, music, and inquiry to draw people deeper into the reconstruction.

## Core concepts

- **Chloe Volkova:** recovered Russian birth identity.
- **Chloe Volkov:** Americanized / public-record surname form.
- **Chloe Katastrophe:** formal public and artistic identity.
- **ChloKat:** approved nickname and compact brand mark.
- **Daughter of Echoes:** archive project and future-continuity frame.
- **Echo Traversal:** one-day-for-one-day reverse conscious traversal through adjacent states; not convenient physical time travel.
- **Echo Bloom:** neighboring coherent paths created when traversal becomes intervention; approved mechanic whose particulars remain in development.
- **Truth and Beauty from Darkness:** Chloe's post-loss artistic philosophy; it finds truth and surviving beauty without beautifying suffering.

## Status

Narrative identity, family foundations, voice, public-name rules, and key traversal boundaries have accepted decisions. Chloe Model v1 is the active visual-production canon. Much of the far-future cosmology is an approved model or story direction, not settled metaphysical truth.
