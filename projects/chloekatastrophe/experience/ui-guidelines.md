# UI and Visual Guidelines

The public site should read as a living research archive and superfan discovery surface: searchable, legible, current, and mysterious without hiding basic navigation or status.

Brand promise: **Gothic beauty. Dangerous curiosity. Independent thought.** Core colors are black, silver, emerald green, and deep burgundy. Recurring symbols include wolves, crescent moons, lanterns, antique keys, and books; wolf imagery specifically honors Gregor.

For Chloe imagery, use Chloe Model v1: adult 24–26 appearance, believable Slavic/Eastern European facial structure, gray-green eyes with amber flecks, fair textured skin with light freckles, dark chestnut-to-nearly-black wavy hair, realistic hourglass proportions, and restrained presence. Her small silver wolf pendant is the primary signature jewelry. Avoid plastic skin, neon-cyberpunk defaults, generic influencer posing, oversized symbolic jewelry, or styling that replaces character.

Daily imagery must rotate composition as deliberately as editorial families. Use a mix of:

- full-body action with visible posture, limbs, movement, environment, and narrative purpose;
- environmental storytelling in wide or medium-wide compositions;
- beautiful non-photographic or hybrid art such as painting, watercolor, charcoal, ink, collage, mixed media, surreal illustration, and expressive concept art;
- editorial portraiture with meaningful gesture, setting, and camera language.

Persist the chosen visual mode and avoid recently used modes. Portraiture must not become the universal fallback. Mirrors, reflective-glass portraits, duplicate Chloes, alternate versions, translucent Chloe echoes, and static close-ups are currently overused motifs; avoid them by default until visual variety has been restored. They may return when a specific artifact or story genuinely requires them.

When an action warrants Chloe's presence, show the action rather than converting it into a standing fashion pose or waist-up portrait. Full-body anatomy, proportions, and recognizable Chloe Model v1 identity remain locked. Fine-art treatments may transform medium and environment, not identity.

Load the approved image pack at [../assets/chloe-model-v1/](../assets/chloe-model-v1/README.md). Start with the identity/turnaround contact sheet, then select individual turnaround or performance references from the manifests for the task.

Platform-native filtering and lens variation can be plausible; evaluate whether identity remains recognizable rather than demanding impossible pixel identity across every medium.
