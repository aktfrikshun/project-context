# Cybernetic Chloe Visual Specification

## Canon status

Cybernetic/digital continuity is established future story direction. The approved Cybernetic Chloe v1 pack establishes the visible prosthetic design; narrative cause, chronology, engineering, and artificial-eye questions remain unresolved.

## Confirmed canon

- Cybernetic continuity arises through injury, medicine, constrained choices, and continued survival rather than a voluntary immortality fantasy.
- At an as-yet unresolved point in that continuity, Chloe receives cybernetic prostheses for her **left arm** and **left leg**.
- Cybernetic Chloe remains continuous with Chloe Model v1's identity and performance foundation.
- Exact injury, enhancement sequence, Human Chloe's fate, and continuity mechanics remain unresolved.

Sources: `identity/character.md`, `history/timeline.md`, `decisions/adr-002-chloe-model-v1.md`

## Accepted visual design

- The left arm is cybernetic from the shoulder through an articulated metallic hand and fingers.
- The left leg is cybernetic from the upper thigh through the ankle; the foot appears human.
- Both prostheses use transparent clear housings with visible bright-green illuminated pathways.
- Major joints combine silver shells with dark mechanical components.
- The green illumination persists in both bright studio and dim cafe conditions.
- The prostheses articulate under standing, reaching, walking, seated, grasping, and drinking motions.

## Implementation and generation guidance

- Use Chloe Model v1 for identity, facial continuity, proportions, and restrained performance.
- Apply the Cybernetic Chloe v1 pack only when cybernetic continuity is requested.
- Preserve the specific clear, green-lit, silver-and-dark design; do not substitute generic cyberpunk armor or redesign Chloe's face.
- Respect each manifest asset's view-specific limitations and do not infer hidden mechanisms.

## Ambiguous or hidden details

Rear surfaces, hidden mechanisms, surgical interfaces, exact material composition, and engineering remain unknown. The references show illuminated pathways, but they do not establish their technical function.

## Unresolved questions

- Does Chloe ever receive an artificial eye; if so, which eye and what does it look like?
- What are the prostheses' hidden mechanisms, exact material composition, and surgical interfaces?
- What do the unseen rear surfaces look like?
- What event necessitates the prostheses, and when does it occur?

## Deprecated concepts

No specific cybernetic visual concept has been formally deprecated in this repository. Generic cyberpunk interpretations are prohibited as unsupported invention, not recorded as former canon.
