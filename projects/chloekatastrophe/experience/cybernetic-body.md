# Cybernetic Chloe Visual Specification

## Canon status

Cybernetic/digital continuity is established future story direction. The approved Cybernetic Chloe v1 pack establishes the visible prosthetic design and artificial-left-eye treatment, governed by ADR-006. Narrative cause, chronology, and engineering remain unresolved.

## Confirmed canon

- Cybernetic continuity arises through injury, medicine, constrained choices, and continued survival rather than a voluntary immortality fantasy.
- At an as-yet unresolved point in that continuity, Chloe receives cybernetic prostheses for her **anatomical left arm** and **anatomical left leg**.
- Chloe's **left eye is artificial**, expressed as a subtle, slightly brighter emerald-green glimmer relative to her natural right eye.
- Cybernetic Chloe remains continuous with Chloe Model v1's identity and performance foundation.
- Exact injury, enhancement sequence, Human Chloe's fate, and continuity mechanics remain unresolved.

Sources: `identity/character.md`, `history/timeline.md`, `decisions/adr-002-chloe-model-v1.md`

## Accepted visual design

- Chloe's anatomical left arm is cybernetic from the shoulder joint through the fingertips, including the hand and every finger.
- Chloe's anatomical left leg is cybernetic from the hip joint through the toes, including the entire thigh, knee, lower leg, ankle, foot, and toes.
- In frontal and near-frontal views, Chloe's anatomical left prostheses and artificial left eye appear on the **viewer's right**.
- Chloe's anatomical right arm, right leg, and right eye remain natural.
- Both prostheses use transparent clear housings with visible bright-green illuminated pathways.
- Major joints combine silver shells with dark mechanical components.
- The green illumination persists in both bright studio and dim cafe conditions.
- The prostheses articulate under standing, reaching, walking, seated, grasping, and drinking motions.
- The artificial left eye preserves Chloe's established eye identity; its only approved visible distinction is a subtle, slightly brighter emerald-green glimmer.

## Implementation and generation guidance

- Use Chloe Model v1 for identity, facial continuity, proportions, and restrained performance.
- Apply the Cybernetic Chloe v1 pack only when cybernetic continuity is requested.
- Preserve anatomical laterality. Do not mirror or transpose the prostheses: in frontal and near-frontal images they must remain on the viewer's right.
- Preserve the full extent of the prosthetic leg. Do not depict a natural left thigh, knee, lower leg, ankle, foot, or toes, and do not begin the prosthesis at the knee or upper thigh.
- Do not add right-side cybernetics or any cybernetic anatomy beyond the accepted left arm, left leg, and subtle artificial left eye.
- Preserve the specific clear, green-lit, silver-and-dark design; do not substitute generic cyberpunk armor or redesign Chloe's face.
- Render the artificial left eye subtly. Do not turn the glimmer into a conspicuous glowing lens, mechanical socket, or facial redesign.
- Respect each manifest asset's view-specific limitations and do not infer hidden mechanisms.

## Visual acceptance check

Reject a generated image unless all visible anatomy satisfies these conditions:

- Viewer's right arm: cybernetic from shoulder through fingertips.
- Viewer's right leg: cybernetic from hip through toes, including the foot.
- Viewer's left arm and leg: fully natural.
- Viewer's right eye: the subtle artificial-left-eye glimmer when the eyes are visible.
- No additional cybernetics.

For rear or strongly rotated views, validate Chloe's anatomical left side rather than relying only on viewer-relative position.

## Ambiguous or hidden details

Rear surfaces, hidden mechanisms, surgical interfaces, exact material composition, and engineering remain unknown. The references show illuminated pathways, but they do not establish their technical function. The approved eye headshot establishes visible appearance only, not the eye's hidden mechanism or surgical interface.

## Unresolved questions

- What are the prostheses' hidden mechanisms, exact material composition, and surgical interfaces?
- What do the unseen rear surfaces look like?
- What event necessitates the prostheses, and when does it occur?

## Deprecated concepts

Conspicuous glowing lenses, exposed mechanical eye sockets, robotic facial redesigns, right-side prostheses, and partial left-leg prostheses beginning below the hip are deprecated. Generic cyberpunk interpretations remain prohibited as unsupported invention.
