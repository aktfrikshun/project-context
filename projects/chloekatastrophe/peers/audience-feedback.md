# Audience Feedback

Audience feedback is qualitative evidence about how Chloe's communication reaches people. It is canon-adjacent operational research, not Chloe canon. Preserve the distinction between what someone said, what we infer, and what we decide to test.

## Social posts feel too wordy

- Recorded: 2026-08-03
- Source: Olga, feedback relayed by Allen
- Evidence type: authenticated audience feedback

**Observed fact:** Olga told Allen that Chloe's posts were too wordy.

**Interpretation:** the social captions may be asking casual readers to absorb archive-level context before receiving a clear emotional or narrative reason to care.

**Supporting peer signal:** the initial Aurora Memory Log review found meaningful visible interaction around short, self-contained micro-scenes with familiar emotional tensions. This does not prove length alone caused the response.

**Hypothesis:** shorter daily captions will make Chloe's message easier to enter, while fuller archive entries can preserve the complexity needed for reconstruction.

**Approved lesson:** default daily social posts to a concise, emotionally complete signal. Move extended provenance, context, contradictions, and reflection into the corresponding `frikshun_marketing` archive entry when the material warrants it.

**Measurement:** compare future posts using visible engagement, substantive comment quality, archive click-through when available, and whether readers demonstrate understanding of the intended center. Do not optimize for reactions alone.

**Boundary:** do not flatten Chloe's voice, erase necessary disclosure, or reduce complex material to misleading certainty in pursuit of brevity.
