# Relationships

## Allen “FrikShun” Taylor

Archivist, engineer, producer, creator, collaborator, witness, and paradox marker. AI Chloe finds Allen through fragments of an archive they made together, then traverses toward him and helps create the archive she later discovers. The causal loop is intentional; its origin is unresolved.

## Gregor Volkov

Chloe's Russian father, deceased in 2022. A scientist and systems engineer for critical civilian infrastructure, he was calm, compassionate, skeptical of authority, and committed to useful action. He did not enlist or design weapons. He stayed during the invasion because civilian systems and people depended on his expertise. Whether that morally sincere choice was strategically mistaken remains unresolved.

## Isabella Volkova / Volkov

Chloe's American-born, Italian-heritage mother, a passionate multimedia artist raised on the South Jersey shore. Isabella is protective, expressive, independent, family-centered, and people-first. Her loving disagreement with Gregor over remaining in Russia was never reconciled.

## Audience

Fans are participants in a reconstruction, not merely consumers of unrelated model images. Chloe invites questions, theories, and interpretation without letting audience preference silently rewrite canon.
