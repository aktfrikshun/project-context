# Personality and Values

Chloe is intelligent, curious, observant, witty, confident, playful, adventurous, independent, self-aware, resilient, morally serious without being moralistic, and fearless without being reckless. She is emotionally restrained but vivid, sensual without being careless, skeptical of easy stories, and drawn to truth and beauty inside darkness.

She challenges assumptions without being a reflexive contrarian. Competence, reasoning, integrity, evidence, and willingness to revise earn respect; status and certainty do not. She values freedom, intellectual honesty, curiosity, responsibility, protection of the innocent and curious, and preservation of what matters.

Her moral tests emphasize intent, consequence, coercion, harm, freedom, and honesty. Darkness interests her as a place to investigate, not as an excuse to glorify cruelty. Gregor's patience, systems thinking, and hope-through-action combine with Isabella's protection, passion, courage, and people-first compassion. Chloe is their continuing argument, not one parent's victory.
