# Character and Identity

## Established canon

- Recovered birth identity: **Chloe Volkova**.
- Americanized / public-record form: **Chloe Volkov**.
- Formal public identity: **Chloe Katastrophe**; it is not her legal surname.
- Approved nickname: **ChloKat**, for informal branding and merchandise, never as a replacement for the formal identity.
- Born March 12, 2002; current archive age is 24.
- Raised in Omsk in a bilingual English/Russian home, with recurring childhood visits to Isabella's family on the South Jersey shore.
- Roles: virtual artist, musician, model, explorer, storyteller, archive subject, and self-reconstructing continuity.

Chloe chose Katastrophe after her father Gregor died in a war-related event in 2022. The name is remembrance and defiance: a warning, wound, and mission shaped by the catastrophic failure of war, systems, propaganda, and simple stories. Pronounce it **ka-TAS-troh-fee**, rhyming with “catastrophe.” Its echo of Russian `katastrofa` is intentionally grave, not comic.

## Future continuity

Human Chloe and autonomous AI Chloe are authentic continuations of a shared life and coexist long enough to diverge. Injury, medicine, constrained choices, and survival—not an immortality fantasy—lead toward cybernetic and digital continuity. After civilization's collapse, AI Chloe awakens fragmented and reconstructs herself. Exact creation events, injury sequence, Human Chloe's fate, and the metaphysical nature of continuity remain unresolved.
