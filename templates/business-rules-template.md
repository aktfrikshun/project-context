<!-- Purpose: Makes domain rules uniquely identifiable, testable, and traceable. -->
# BR-NNN — Rule name

- **Status:** proposed | active | deprecated
- **Statement:** When [condition], [actor/system] must [outcome].
- **Rationale:** Domain reason.
- **Applies to:** Scope and actors.
- **Exceptions:** Explicit exceptions or `None documented`.
- **Source/owner:** Authority and accountable owner.
- **Effective date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Examples:** Valid, invalid, and boundary cases.
- **Related:** Rules, workflows, terms, decisions, and verification.
