# INT-NNN — Integration name

- **Purpose:** Capability exchanged.
- **Direction:** inbound | outbound | bidirectional
- **Owners:** Internal and external.
- **Contract:** Authoritative API, event, file, or process specification.
- **Data classification:** Data categories, residency, and retention.
- **Trust boundary:** Authentication, authorization, encryption, and validation.
- **Reliability:** Timeouts, retries, idempotency, ordering, rate limits, and recovery.
- **Environments:** Symbolic configuration and ownership; no secrets.
- **Observability:** Metrics, logs, alerts, health checks, and support route.
- **Lifecycle:** Versioning, compatibility, deprecation, and exit plan.
- **Related:** Architecture, workflows, rules, and ADRs.
