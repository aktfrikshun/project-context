# Shared glossary

Terms are sorted alphabetically. Project-specific meanings belong in the project's glossary and may not silently redefine a shared term.

## Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

A durable record of a significant decision, its context, alternatives, and consequences.

## Authoritative source

The document designated as the maintained source of truth for a fact or rule. Copies and generated views are not authoritative.

## Business rule

A testable constraint or policy originating from the domain, independent of a particular implementation.

## Project context

The curated knowledge needed to understand and responsibly change one project.

## Shared standard

A default expectation that applies to multiple projects unless an explicit exception exists.
